Program overview

Real mentorship, not a lecture series.

Instead of traditional lectures, students gather data and train a real AI model, grasping the core concepts independently. A dedicated mentor clears doubts and guides the process, but the thinking is up to the students.
"The mentorship is in the thinking, not the tooling."

Self-Guided Learning

Grasp concepts independently. There are no passive lectures instead you learn by researching, experimenting, and building on your own.

Team-Based Delivery

Work as a team of 4 to submit one cohesive project report, ensuring every member contributes actively to the presentations.

Weekly Review Presentations

Defend choices and share progress in weekly team presentations, receiving direct feedback and course correction from your mentor.

AI for Agriculture

AI models as practical agritech solutions.

Learning AI is only valuable when applied to real-world datasets. Students focus on solving daily agricultural challenges by training AI models that translate raw data into clear, actionable classifications and decission making models.

This month's Cader Initiative focus is on:

  • 01 Training computer vision models to flag leaf infection and crop disease from photo scans.
  • 02 Developing predictive algorithms to convert soil and weather data into planting recommendations.
  • 03 Deploying real, functional AI models that address actual challenges faced by farmers.

Guidelines

How the program works?

Team Allotment & Ecosystem

  • Allotted Teams: A team will consist of 4 members directly allotted by the mentor. There is no option to choose your teammates or project.
  • Leadership: A Team Lead is appointed by the mentor based on prior experience.
  • Teamwork Value: This is a unique opportunity to work in a different ecosystem with random people to master real-world teamwork.

Methodology & Presentations

  • Self-Guided Grasp: Instead of regular classroom teaching, students have to grasp concepts and work on their own, guided and supported by a mentor for clearing doubts.
  • Weekly Reviews: Teams must deliver weekly presentation reviews.
  • Final Submission: Deliver a final presentation and submit one collaborative team report where all members contribute.

Registration & Fees

  • Post-Confirmation Payment: Register for free today. Payment is only required after confirmation and joining the official WhatsApp group.
  • ₹150 Registration: The ₹150 fee covers the standard 1-month duration of the project.
  • Extension Terms: If the project goes beyond 1 month, there is a 1-week grace period (no extra fee). If it goes beyond 5 weeks, the whole team must pay ₹150 each.

Certification & Incentives

  • Company Certification: Students receive a formal training completion certificate from NoxusDynamics.
  • Research Paper Backing: If the final outcome is outstanding, the team can write a research paper with the full backing of NoxusDynamics.
  • Hiring & Internships: Exceptional work gives students a direct opportunity to get hired by the company or secure a free internship.
  • IP Access: NoxusDynamics retains full rights to access and utilize the models and works produced.
